# Code of conduct

Be kind. This is a project about a small duck.

- Treat everyone here with respect, whatever their experience or background.
- Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- No harassment, slurs, or personal attacks, here or anywhere else about this project.
- Keep private information private. Do not post other people's details.

If someone crosses a line, report it privately to the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are read and handled quietly. The maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or block people who break these rules.
