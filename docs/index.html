A robot duck that lives on your Mac.

It walks along the bottom of your screen, climbs your windows, falls over and gets back up, and talks back.

Download for Mac View on GitHub

macOS 12 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel · Unofficial, inspired by Pollen's Microduck

It does things.

It also talks back through Claude, opens apps and looks things up if you let it, keeps your Mac awake when you ask, naps, sulks when ignored, likes being petted and chases a beach ball.

Chat needs a brain

Chat runs through Claude: your Claude Code CLI if it is installed, or an Anthropic API key. Without either, it still walks, climbs and plays. It just can't talk back.

Private by design

No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry. A new duck runs nothing on your Mac. It checks only whether a microphone is in use, never the sound. Everything it reads and sends is listed in the README.

A real robot, in simulation

Under the feathers it is Pollen Robotics' Microduck: their MuJoCo model and their trained walking policies, from the open source Microduck simulator by Pollen Robotics and Hugging Face. Every step is a trained robot policy driving a physics simulation, not an animation.

Digital Duck is an independent project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pollen Robotics or Hugging Face. If you like the duck, get the real one.
